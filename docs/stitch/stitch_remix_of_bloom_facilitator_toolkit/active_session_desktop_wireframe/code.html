Phonemic Blending Exercise

Instruct students to listen carefully as you say the sounds of a word slowly. For example, say "/c/ /a/ /t/". Ask the students to blend the sounds together to guess the word ("cat").

lightbulb Teaching Tip

Use physical gestures, like tapping your arm for each sound and sweeping down to blend, to help kinesthetic learners.

Try using a visual analogy they are familiar with. You can explain blending like making tea:

  • The water is one sound (/c/)
  • The tea leaves are another (/a/)
  • The sugar is the last (/t/)

When you put them together (blend them), they make something new: a cup of sweet tea ("cat").
