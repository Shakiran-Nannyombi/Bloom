Body Autonomy & Boundaries

Prepare yourself with key concepts before initiating this important conversation with youth.

pan_tool

Definition

Body autonomy means a person has the right to govern their own body. It teaches youth they are in charge of who touches them and how.

verified_user

Why It Matters

Establishing boundaries early helps protect youth from abuse and empowers them to communicate discomfort confidently.

Anticipated Questions from Youth

"What if an adult I know wants to hug me?" expand_more

Guide them to understand that they can say "no" even to family members. Suggest alternatives like high-fives or waving if they prefer not to hug.

"Is it rude to say no?" expand_more

Reassure them that setting a boundary is never rude; it is a healthy way to respect their own comfort levels.
