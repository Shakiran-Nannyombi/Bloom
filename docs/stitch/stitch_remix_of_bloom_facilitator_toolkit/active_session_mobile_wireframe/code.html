The Sounds of 'A'

Start by writing the capital letter 'A' and lowercase 'a' on the chalkboard. Point to them clearly.

Say: "Today we are learning the sound this letter makes. It says /a/ as in Apple."

tips_and_updates Teacher Tip

Ensure students are watching your mouth as you pronounce the sound. Have them repeat it together 3 times.
